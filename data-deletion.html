Data Deletion

Request removal of your personal data.

1. Your Right to Erasure

Under applicable privacy laws, you have the right to request the deletion of your personal data that we have collected and maintained about you ("Right to be Forgotten"). We are committed to complying with such requests, subject to certain exceptions outlined by law (such as retaining information necessary for security, fraud prevention, or fulfilling legal obligations).

2. What Information Can Be Deleted?

Upon your verified request, we will delete data such as:

  • Your profile and account information
  • Your past booking and reservation history (after any necessary statutory retention period)
  • Your marketing preferences and email newsletter subscription

3. How to Request Data Deletion

To request the deletion of your personal data, please fill out the form below. Once submitted, we will process your request within 30 days. We may need to contact you to verify your identity before completing the deletion.

4. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

If you have any questions regarding your data, privacy, or this deletion process, please contact our Data Protection Officer at theplayhousecbe@gmail.com.
