Welcome to Playhouse

More than just a cafe. We are a community hub dedicated to bringing people together over great food and unforgettable games.

Our Story

Born from a love of cardboard and coffee.

Playhouse Cafe started with a simple idea: in a world increasingly dominated by screens, people are craving genuine, face-to-face connections. We realized there was no better catalyst for conversation and laughter than a fantastic board game.

We've curated a library of hundreds of games. Whether you're a seasoned tabletop veteran or looking for a fun date night activity, our Game Masters are always on hand to recommend and teach you the perfect game.

What We Stand For

Community First

We provide a safe, inclusive space where strangers become friends and friends become family over the roll of a dice.

Premium Quality

From our artisan coffee to our loaded fries and carefully maintained game library, we never compromise on quality.

Unplug & Play

Put down the phone and pick up some cards. We encourage digital detoxing for real-world fun.
